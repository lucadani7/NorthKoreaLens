# The team

Here you can see who makes NK Lens and who is responsible for what we publish. Each member of the team chooses how they appear here: with their real name, a first name or a pseudonym.

## Join us

NK Lens is a one-person project for now. If you would like to help, see [how you can help](/how-to-help). The code is open source on [GitHub](https://github.com/lucadani7/NorthKoreaLens).
