# How you can help

NK Lens is a small, non-commercial project. Here is how you can help it grow.

## Read and share

The simplest way to help is to read NK Lens and share the articles you find useful. Every new reader makes it a little harder for propaganda to go unanswered.

## Tell us about mistakes

If a translation sounds wrong, a label seems unfair or an article is missing context, please tell us. Corrections from readers make the site better for everyone.

## Help with translations

NK Lens is available in nine languages, and some translations have not yet been reviewed by native speakers. If you speak Romanian, Korean, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese or Turkish and would like to review a few pages, we would be glad of your help.

The code of NK Lens is open source on [GitHub](https://github.com/lucadani7/NorthKoreaLens), under the Apache 2.0 licence. Translations live in plain files there, so you can also suggest corrections directly.

## Join the discussions

Once discussions open, you will be able to ask questions, share what you know and add context to the news. Please be kind, stick to facts and never share details that could put someone in danger.

## Stay safe

If you have a personal connection to North Korea, think about your safety and your family's before sharing anything. You can use a pseudonym on NK Lens, and you never have to tell us who you are.
